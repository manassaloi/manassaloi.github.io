The best marketing for an AI tool: give a great creator unlimited credits and get out of the way.

[@tupacabra](https://x.com/tupacabra) made *Nuremberg* with [@AskVenice](https://x.com/AskVenice), a trailer for a movie that doesn't exist. It has got 28m+ views till now. It is so good that people are asking Grok if it's a real movie.

And Venice has hit an all time high in video generations. No amount of paid marketing could have got them that.

[@WisprFlow](https://x.com/WisprFlow) should sponsor Robert Greene's next book.

After his stroke, when typing got hard, he wrote *The Law of the Sublime* by hand, and it took him seven years.

A NYT bestseller written by voice is about the best marketing a dictation app could get.
