Foundation model labs have a massive moat problem. And that is the reason ANT stock is crashing now that Gemini Neon is out and insiders are dumping.

The only thing keeping users on a subscription is one question: "Is your model currently the best?" The moment you slip, people churn.

Here's how that played out at my company over the last few months:

We started with Claude plans for everyone. Then Anthropic stumbled. Fable and the rest of the lineup just weren't cutting it. Everyone wanted Codex with Astra instead, so we bought Codex too.

Then Anthropic dropped Opus 5.5, and it was legitimately great. Suddenly nobody needed to use Fable or Astra anymore. Even though we still had both plans, the team gravitated right back to Opus.

Personally, I was paying for ChatGPT and Claude Opus. Then Google dropped Gemini 4 Neon. My stack shifted overnight:

* Opus 5.5 for coding, design, and heavy lifting
* Grok for marketing and insights (most of our users are on X, and Grok is the only one with native X data)
* Gemini Neon for jamming. It actually talks like a human, and its coding is right up there with Opus 5.5

So what was I even doing with my ChatGPT subscription? Nothing. I cancelled it.

Most people aren't going to pay for 3+ AI subscriptions. The ceiling for frontier models for professional work is probably 2. Unless OpenAI drops something so good that it forces people to cancel Claude or Gemini, they just lose the slot.

And that's the real threat to standalone labs like Anthropic and OpenAI. All your organisational context is in markdown files. Skills are files too. If the next Gemini drop clearly beats Opus, we will happily cancel Claude too.

Most companies are already paying for Google Workspace anyway. Bundling wins when model quality reaches parity.

Grok will survive for a core group of users because it owns a proprietary data moat (X). Google has distribution and ecosystem lock-in.

If you're a standalone lab, "having the frontier model" isn't a moat. It's a treadmill.

You need something that stops users from cancelling the exact week you aren't number 1.
