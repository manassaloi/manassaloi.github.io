title:  "No Answer Key"
---

Math has an answer.

And code compiles or it doesn't.

Both have an answer key. If a machine can grade it, a machine can do it.

Creative tasks have no answer key. Give two great designers the same brief and you'll get two completely different outputs. You can't prove which one is better. You can only feel it. And it depends on your taste.

Yesterday I cut a video. I spent a lot of time sampling videos before that. The video ended up with blocky text, fast frames, and what I call pure TikTok energy. I loved it.

I sent it to our designer, someone with years of experience and a great eye for motion design, for a quick polish. He came back with a completely different take.

I actually liked mine more. We posted his anyway.

The point isn't who was right. It's that nobody could prove who was right. There is no answer key.

In creative work, the moment a format works, everyone copies it. Then everyone copies the copies. A template's strength decays exponentially toward slop.

Instrument Serif is a beautiful typeface. Now it's on every seed-stage landing page.

So someone always has to keep inventing. New formats. New angles. There's no large dataset of "taste." AI trains on consensus. Taste requires breaking it.

Anything with an objective answer will get automated. Anything that runs on taste is here to stay.

Recommended watch: Designing a composer by Pablo Stanley.
